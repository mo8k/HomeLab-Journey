OPEN THE PAGE
1. If you downloaded the ZIP, extract the whole ZIP first.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari.
3. Keep index.html, styles.css, script.js, and the assets folder together.
No installation, internet connection, or local server is required.

PRESENT AT THE PANEL
- Click "Present journey" at the top of the page.
- Move through the overview and seven stages with Previous / Next, arrow keys,
  or Page Up / Page Down. Home returns to the overview. End shows the last stage.
- Click Fullscreen if available. Presentation mode also works in a normal window.
- Press Escape or click Exit presentation to return to the timeline.
- Presentation begins at the section nearest your current scrolling position.
- On small screens or short windows, a stage may need vertical scrolling.
- For projector use, start with browser zoom at 100% and a 16:9 display.

EDIT THE STORY
- index.html contains the text, stage order, image captions, and image paths.
- styles.css contains the layout and colors.
- script.js contains presentation controls and stage navigation.
- Images in assets are original photos extracted from Mohamed_Kamate_HomeLab.pdf
  or converted from the supplied IMG_3175.heic. They are not AI-generated.
